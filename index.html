Frequently Asked Questions

What does Astonish Property Group do?

Astonish Property Group develops and refurbishes high-end homes. We build new residential homes, renovate existing houses and flats to a luxury standard and source premium kitchens from Pedini, Binova, Miton and Leicht.

Where does Astonish Property Group work?

We are based in London and work across London and Hertfordshire. Recent projects include East Ham, Highgate and Brondesbury Park in London and The Walled Garden in Preston, Hertfordshire.

How much experience does Astonish Property Group have?

The business is founder-led and built on 35 years of refurbishing flats and houses, from Victorian terraces to Art Deco mansion flats, alongside ground-up luxury development.

Can you source a Pedini, Binova or Leicht kitchen for my home?

Yes. We source Pedini, Binova, Miton and Leicht kitchens. Email sat@astonishproperty.com with your plans and we will take the sourcing off your hands.

What is The Walled Garden?

The Walled Garden is our featured ground-up development: a one-of-a-kind family home set within the grounds of a historic walled estate in Preston, Hertfordshire, combining contemporary architecture with its heritage setting.

How do I contact Astonish Property Group?
